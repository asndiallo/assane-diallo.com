## Live checks (2026-11-12, searcher near San Antonio, TX)

**"auto repair san antonio"**

- No paid listings block. The map list's first slot was an ad; then three shops with **64,
  111 and 121 reviews**, categories "Auto repair shop" and "Mechanic". Much smaller numbers
  than plumbing or HVAC: a shop with a hundred honest reviews competes.
- Review lines: trust and speed ("reliable mechanic with excellent customer service", "within
  an hour our vehicle was repaired", a mobile mechanic who "came to my apartment").
- "Have AI get prices" button.
- Web results: Reddit threads "looking for a trustworthy mechanic" (first web result), a AAA
  "Approved Auto Repair" directory, Yelp's "Best 10". Shop sites' snippets lead with
  credentials: "ASE Certified, AAA Approved, BBB Accredited", years in business, state
  inspection.

**"brake repair near me"**

- A paid listings block (Local Services Ads) for "Brake Shops": rating, years in business,
  "Free consultation", "Book online".
- The map list used the narrower **"Brake shop"** category, plus a mobile brake service
  (5.0, 293 reviews, no address: a service-area business). Review lines name the job: "front
  and rear brake job", "new brake line within 20 minutes".
- People also ask: "How much should a brake job cost?", "What does a $99 brake special
  include?"

**"auto repair near me"**

- Five shops 0.9–3.1 km away with ratings, addresses, phones; **no review counts** this time.
  Categories on the cards: "Mechanic", "Auto repair shop", "Tire shop".
- Each line describes **what the shop does**: "independent mechanic, general repairs and
  maintenance", "full-service repairs, A/C, cooling systems and maintenance", "tires, brakes,
  batteries, alignments and routine maintenance", "general repairs and A/C service".
- "Ratings and distances are based on available listings; verify before visiting."
- It then asked **which vehicle** (it remembered the owner's car), **what repair** (engine /
  check-engine light, oil change, brakes, transmission, suspension, AC/heating, tires /
  alignment, inspection) and **what matters most** (affordable and trustworthy, quality,
  fastest), and said it would find "the best shop for the actual problem, not just the closest
  one".
- Under the answer: an **ad** from a tool and parts retailer (jump starters, chargers).

## Changes to the plan (from the vertical page and the checks)

1. **Services by repair and by make:** list each repair (brakes, check-engine diagnosis, A/C,
   transmission, tires/alignment, state inspection if you're a station) and the makes you work
   on or don't; ChatGPT matches the problem to the shop.
2. **Category:** "Auto repair shop" or "Mechanic", plus narrower ones only if true (brake shop,
   tire shop, transmission, body shop).
3. **Trust signals:** certifications only if true (ASE, AAA Approved), warranty on parts and
   labor, written estimates, showing old parts; these are what Reddit asks about and what
   shop sites lead with.
4. **Reviews:** a hundred honest reviews competes here. Add the FTC rule (fake reviews, buying
   reviews conditioned on sentiment, relatives) next to Google's rules.
5. **Mobile mechanics:** service-area rules (hide the address if customers don't come to you).
6. **Prices:** "$99 brake special" searches: if you run a special, say exactly what it
   includes; ChatGPT and Google both look for price info.
7. **Ads:** ChatGPT and Google both show ads around the answer; free profile stays the base.
